Our Story

A Treasure Trove for Book Lovers

Nestled in the heart of Ahmedabad at 16 Seller Kanak Chamber, Sagar Book Centre has been a beloved destination for readers since 1982. Walk in and immerse yourself in the scent of new pages as you browse a diverse collection spanning every genre imaginable.

Whether you're on the hunt for a thrilling novel, a timeless classic, or the latest release, our friendly staff are always on hand to help you find your next great read.
